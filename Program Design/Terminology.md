# Terminology

## General

- **Generalisation** - Making a solution applicable not just in a concrete case, but useful for more general cases
- **Abstraction** - Hiding the details of a solution, only making available information important in the current context
- **Low/High level**
	- Low level - closer to machine level
	- High level - closer to human level

## Pillars of Computational Thinking

- **Decomposition** - break a complex problem into manageable parts
- **Pattern recognition** - notice similarities that may let us reuse a solution
- **Abstraction** - keep what matters; suppress irrelevant detail
- **Algorithms** - express a solution as precise, finite steps
