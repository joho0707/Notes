# Computers and Technology

## Information Use

- People have invented many ways of storing information and manipulating it.
- Most obvious examples is writing using alphabetic characters and numbers
- Characters, numbers etc. represented on stone, wood, etc... now computers.
- Information in books is static (does not change) - some info changes over time (quick or slow)

### Numbers as Binary

Base 10 number system, decimal, uses ten number representations:

- 0 - 9

Base 2 number system, binary, uses two number representations:

- 0 and 1

Binary is used to mimic decimal numbers, called **binary representation**

## Computers

Computers are used for a variety of tasks:

- Looking up information and news
- Exchanging e-mail
- Shopping and paying bills
- Watching TV and videos
- Downloading music and movies
- Organising digital photographs
- Playing games
- Telecommuting (Working from home)

Considerations for computers as information systems:

- Speed
- Reliability
- Consistency
- Storage
- Communication
